Contact mQuickCalc — send feedback, report a calculation error, or suggest a new tool.

We'd love to hear from you

Whether you are a professional who spotted an issue, a student with a question, or a partner with an idea — every message is read.

Operator

mQuickCalc is operated by Jim (Maoming, Guangdong, China). For any legal or privacy request you can reach the operator at feedback@mquickcalc.com.

Email

feedback@mquickcalc.com — general feedback, corrections and suggestions.

What to include

  • Which calculator/page you are writing about (URL helps a lot);
  • What you expected and what happened (for bug reports);
  • Any published source or reference (for correction reports from professionals).

What happens next

  1. We triage feedback within a few business days.
  2. Corrections to medical/financial logic are treated as highest priority and, once verified against the published source, are pushed promptly.
  3. We reply personally to questions that need one.

Privacy

Messages are kept only to answer your inquiry and are not added to marketing lists. See our Privacy Policy.

We currently set no tracking or advertising cookies. A single non-identifying preference is stored on your device to remember this notice. If advertising or analytics partners that set cookies are added later, we will ask for your consent first. See our Cookie notice and Privacy Policy.
