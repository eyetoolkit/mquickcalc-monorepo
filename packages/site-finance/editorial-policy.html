Editorial policy

Last reviewed: 2026-11-16 · Operator: Jim (Maoming, Guangdong, China)

This policy covers every page on mQuickCalc Finance. It identifies who is responsible for the content and how we keep it accurate.

Who writes and reviews

All tools and explanations are written and reviewed by the operator, Jim, named on our About page. This is a one-person independent project — no editorial board, no outside contributor.

Accuracy and limitations

Calculators here are estimates derived from published formulas and public schedules. They are not financial, medical or legal advice. Always verify against the official provider or a qualified professional before making a decision that costs money or affects health.

How a tool is published

  1. Inputs, output and edge cases are specified before code.
  2. The tool runs entirely in your browser — no input is uploaded.
  3. Calculation logic is checked against known cases before publishing.
  4. Copy is reviewed against the actual implementation.
  5. Pages are deployed statically; no server step alters content.

Corrections

If a result is wrong, report it through contact with the tool name and sample input. Corrections to calculation logic are treated as priority. We do not silently rewrite published figures.

Advertising and measurement

Where advertising appears it is served in clearly marked slots by third-party networks; ad content is not written by us and never changes a result. We use cookieless aggregate analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) and a first-party anonymous visit counter; neither receives your tool input. See Privacy Policy.
