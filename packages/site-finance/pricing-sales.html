6 free calculators & converters. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded.

Available calculators

Markup & Margin Calculator

Turn a product cost into the right selling price by markup % or target margin %, and see both markup and margin at once. Free, no sign-up.

Sales Commission Calculator

Calculate commission earned on a sale, total earnings with a base, or how much you must sell to hit an income target. Free, no sign-up.

Sales Tax Calculator

Add sales tax to a price or back the tax out of an inclusive amount. Pick a US state rate or enter your own. Free, no sign-up.

Discount Calculator

Find the sale price after a discount or reverse it to recover the original price from a discounted price. Free, no sign-up.

VAT Calculator

Add or remove VAT on any amount in one click. Enter a net or gross figure, choose your rate, and see the VAT and the other figure instantly. Free.

Convert between hourly wage and annual salary. Account for overtime, unpaid time off, and weeks worked per year. Free, no sign-up.
