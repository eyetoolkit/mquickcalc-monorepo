Frequently asked questions

How do marketplace fee calculators work?

They take your sale price (and sometimes shipping or item cost) and apply each platform’s published fee schedule — listing, transaction, payment-processing and offsite-ad fees — then show your net payout. Nothing is uploaded; the math runs entirely in your browser.

Why do the same fees differ between platforms?

Each marketplace sets its own percentages, fixed fees and promo/offsites. For example Etsy charges a transaction plus payment fee and an optional 15% offsite-ads fee, while eBay uses final-value-fee tiers. A comparison tool shows the difference per sale.

Which selling fees can I usually deduct?

In many jurisdictions, marketplace selling fees, payment-processing fees and shipping are ordinary business expenses for sellers operating as a business. Rules vary by location and situation, so confirm with a qualified tax professional.
