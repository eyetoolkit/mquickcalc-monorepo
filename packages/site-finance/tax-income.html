Frequently asked questions

How do I estimate my take-home pay?

Enter your gross salary or hourly rate, pay frequency, state and filing status. The paycheck and income-tax calculators subtract federal (and where applicable state) tax, Social Security and Medicare to show net pay.

What is the difference between hourly and salary?

Salary is a fixed annual amount divided across pay periods; hourly pay multiplies hours worked by a rate and may include overtime. The hourly-to-salary converter switches between the two.

Do I need to charge VAT on digital products?

If you sell to EU/UK buyers, VAT often applies at the buyer’s country rate (for example Etsy EU sales). The VAT and Etsy EU VAT calculators show the impact on your price. Rules vary — check local guidance.
