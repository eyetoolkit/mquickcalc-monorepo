Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate ROI?

ROI = (gain − cost) / cost × 100%. The ROI & CAGR calculator also annualizes returns into a compound annual growth rate so different holding periods are comparable.

What is the difference between NPV and IRR?

NPV discounts future cash flows to today using a chosen rate; a positive NPV means the investment beats that rate. IRR is the discount rate that makes NPV zero — your effective return. The NPV & IRR calculator shows both.

How does compound interest grow savings?

Each period’s interest earns interest the next period. The savings and compound-interest calculators show the curve; adding regular monthly contributions accelerates it.
