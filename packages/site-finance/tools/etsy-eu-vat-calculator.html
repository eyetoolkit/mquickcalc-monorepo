VAT on Etsy for EU sellers

VAT is charged on the sale price and added to it, so the gross price includes VAT. To find your pre-VAT revenue, divide the gross by (1 + rate). Etsy's transaction and payment fees are charged on the gross order, which is why they matter when pricing.

How Etsy fees are calculated

Etsy charges a listing fee per item, a transaction fee on the sale price, and a payment processing fee. Some categories have different fee structures. Offsite Ads participation may add an additional 12% fee.

How EU VAT works

VAT = Net price times tax rate (varies 17-27% by country). One Stop Shop (OSS) simplifies cross-border filing. Low-value goods (under 150 euro) may use IOSS for quick EU imports. Reverse charge applies to B2B intra-EU sales.

Frequently asked questions

Does Etsy collect VAT for EU sellers?

For digital items sold to EU buyers, Etsy often collects and remits VAT itself. For physical goods, sellers are typically responsible for VAT accounting. Always check current Etsy rules for your items.

How do I remove VAT from an Etsy price?

Divide the VAT-inclusive price by 1 + the rate. A €120 order at 20% VAT is €100 net, with €20 VAT.
