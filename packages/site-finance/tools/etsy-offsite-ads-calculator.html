What offsite ads are

Etsy advertises your listings on Google, Facebook, Instagram and other sites. When a sale is attributed to one of those ads, Etsy charges an ad fee — 15% for sellers under $10k in trailing-12-month sales, 12% for sellers who've passed $10k. Sellers under the threshold can opt out.

How Etsy fees are calculated

Etsy charges a listing fee per item, a transaction fee on the sale price, and a payment processing fee. Some categories have different fee structures. Offsite Ads participation may add an additional 12% fee.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy charge for offsite ads?

15% of an attributed order for sellers under $10k in trailing-12-month sales, or 12% once past $10k. It's charged in addition to regular Etsy fees.

Can I turn off Etsy offsite ads?

Sellers under the $10k threshold can opt out. Once you exceed $10k in sales over the trailing 12 months, offsite ads become mandatory.
