Vinted has no seller commission in most markets; sellers receive the full listed price. Roughly $0.70 (or a small %) is folded into the buyer's price as buyer protection in some countries. Confirm current rules locally.

Why sellers often pick Vinted

Unlike Depop or Poshmark, Vinted doesn't take a percentage of the sale. In most markets you keep the exact price you list. To fund protections and shipping, the buyer protection fee sits on the buyer's side — which is a lighter model for sellers.

How Vinted fees work

Vinted operates on a buyer-protection model where the seller pays a service fee on each sale. The fee structure varies by country but typically ranges from 5% to 19% of the item price, with a minimum fee per transaction.

Unlike Depop or Poshmark, Vinted's fee is automatically deducted from your sale price. There are no listing fees or monthly subscription costs, making it low-risk for casual sellers. However, the fee percentage is one of the highest among second-hand marketplaces.

To maximize profits, consider bundling items to reach higher price points (reducing the percentage impact), pricing for offers (most buyers on Vinted expect discounts), and using Vinted's shipping labels which can sometimes reduce overall costs compared to postal services.

Frequently asked questions

Does Vinted charge sellers a fee?

In most markets, no commission — sellers keep the listed price. Shipping and a buyer protection fee are handled on the buyer's side.

What does a buyer pay on Vinted?

The item price plus shipping, and in many countries a small buyer protection fee. This is why sellers can offer lower net prices than on fee-taking marketplaces.
