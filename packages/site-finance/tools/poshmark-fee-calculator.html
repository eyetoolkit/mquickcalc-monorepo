Poshmark fee structure is a flat $2.95 under $15 or 20% at $15 and above. This does not include redemption or any optional withdrawal fees.

Poshmark fee rule

Items sold for less than $15 incur a flat $2.95 fee. Items sold for $15 or more incur a 20% fee. There is no listing fee, and the buyer normally pays shipping via Poshmark's prepaid label.

How Poshmark seller fees work

Poshmark doesn't charge to list an item. When it sells, its bundled selling fee is deducted from your payout. Because the fee is a flat amount below $15 and a percentage above, margin math works very differently on low-priced items — a $10 sale leaves only $7.05, while a $50 sale keeps $40.

Frequently asked questions

What does Poshmark take on a $100 sale?

A 20% fee of $20, leaving a $80 payout (plus anything subtracted for your own shipping costs).

Is there a Poshmark listing fee?

No. Listing is free and fees are only charged when an item sells.
