Grailed fee basics

Grailed charges a seller commission (often 9%) and payment processing applies on top. There's no listing fee, so your only costs are on a successful sale.

How Grailed fees work

9% seller fee plus PayPal processing (~2.9% + 0.30). Grailed is the leading marketplace for pre-owned designer fashion. Higher-end items may have better margins but slower velocity.

Frequently asked questions

What fees does Grailed charge sellers?

A percentage commission on each sale, commonly 9%, plus payment processing of a few percent. Payout is the sale price minus these fees.

Does Grailed charge to list?

No — listing is free; Grailed takes its cut when the item sells.
