Which platform is right for you?

All four of these secondhand marketplaces take a commission on each sale, but their fee structures differ. Vinted runs on a small fixed fee (no commission), which makes it cheapest for low-priced items. Depop and Mercari each take about 10% plus payment processing. Grailed sits in the middle. Pick based on your category (Depop for streetwear, Mercari for general, Grailed for curated menswear, Vinted for European reach).

How secondhand platform fees work

Secondhand marketplaces charge a seller fee (a percentage of the sale price), plus payment processing for card transactions. Some add a small fixed fee per sale. Whether you absorb shipping is up to you — the calculator subtracts any seller-paid shipping you enter.

Frequently asked questions

Which secondhand platform charges the lowest fees?

Vinted generally has the lowest commission among the four (a small fixed + buyer protection fee, not a percentage), but its buyer pool is mostly Europe. Depop charges about 10% plus payment processing. Mercari and Grailed each take 10% plus payment processing, with Grailed often slightly higher. Pick based on your category, not fee alone.

Do these platforms also charge the buyer?

Depop and Vinted add a buyer-protection fee on top of the seller's fees. Mercari and Grailed generally do not. Total cost to the buyer = listing price + buyer fee, but only the seller's fees are subtracted from your payout in this calculator.
