Why geography matters for RPM

Advertisers pay far more for US, Canada, UK, Australia and Western EU viewers. Two channels with the same views can earn very different money — the one with the richer view mix wins. If most of your audience is low-RPM regions, niche-specific sponsors and memberships often beat pure ad revenue.

How YouTube revenue works

YouTube pays from its ad revenue pool to creators in the Partner Program. AdSense RPM varies by viewer location, content type, and ad demand. This calculator estimates monthly earnings from views and RPM.

Frequently asked questions

What's a good YouTube RPM?

US-heavy finance and tech channels can see $8–20+, while global general content often lands $1–4. Your blended RPM is a weighted average of your viewers' countries.

How do I find my RPM by country?

In YouTube Studio, go to Analytics → Revenue and filter by country. Divide each country's revenue by its views (per 1,000) to get its RPM.
