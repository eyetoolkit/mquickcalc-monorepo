The "charge to net" trick

Business sellers often want to receive a fixed amount after fees. With a fee of rate r and fixed f on gross g, net = g − g·r − f, so the gross that nets target t is t + f over (1 − r). The calculator shows this whenever you set a target net.

How PayPal fees work

Goods and Services: 2.9% plus 0.30 per transaction (US). International transactions add currency conversion fees and cross-border charges. Fees are deducted from your receipt — buyers do not pay extra.

Frequently asked questions

How much does PayPal charge per transaction?

For US commercial or business payments, around 2.99% plus a small fixed fee. International payments and currency conversions cost more.

Can I charge the fee to the customer?

Passing the fee to buyers is against PayPal's merchant terms in most cases. Build the fee into your price instead using the calculator's net-target feature.
