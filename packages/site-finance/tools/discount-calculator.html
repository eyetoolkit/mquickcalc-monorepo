Two ways to use this

Find the sale price: original × (1 − discount). $80 at 20% off is $64. Recover the original: if a $64 price tag is showing 20% off, the original was $64 ÷ 0.8 = $80 — useful for spotting how genuine a "deal" was.

How discount math works

Sale price = Original price times (1 minus discount percent). Reverse: Original price = Sale price divided by (1 minus discount percent). Stacking discounts multiply, they do NOT add. A 10% then 10% discount equals 19% off total.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a sale price from a discount?

Subtract the discount as a decimal from 1 and multiply by the original. 20% off means multiply by 0.80.

How do I find the original price from a discounted price?

Divide the discounted price by (1 − the discount as a decimal). $64 ÷ 0.80 = $80.
