Depop fee basics

Depop charges a selling fee (10% of the item price in many markets) on top of which payment processing fees apply to card transactions. Whether you absorb shipping is up to you — the calculator subtracts any seller-paid shipping you enter.

How Depop fees work

10% seller fee plus payment processing (about 2.9% plus 0.30). Depop focuses on vintage and streetwear. Mobile-first marketplace. Payouts via PayPal typically take 3-5 business days.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Depop charge sellers?

A 10% selling fee is common, plus payment processing of a few percent for card orders. Payout is the sale price minus these fees.

Is Depop free to list on?

Listing is free; fees are charged when an item sells.
