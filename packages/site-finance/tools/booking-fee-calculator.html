How Booking.com fees work

Booking.com's commission varies based on how guests pay. When guests book and pay online (OLB), the commission is higher because Booking handles payment processing. When guests book through Booking.com but pay at the hotel (OTA), the rate is lower. The 15-25% range covers this spectrum.
