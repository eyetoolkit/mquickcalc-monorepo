How Shopify charges

Each plan has a transaction rate (a % plus a fixed $0.30 per online order in the US) when you use Shopify Payments. If you bring your own payment gateway, Shopify adds a plan-specific surcharge percentage instead of the $0.30 fixed fee.

How Shopify fees work

Monthly subscription (Starter to Advanced, 5-399 per month) plus transaction fees (2.9% + 0.30 for external payments, lower for Shopify Payments). Additional fees apply for gift cards, subscriptions, and marketplace apps.

Frequently asked questions

Which Shopify plan has the lowest fees?

Advanced has the lowest transaction rate (2.4% + $0.30 with Shopify Payments) but the highest monthly subscription — ideal for high-volume stores. Basic has the highest per-transaction cost.

Does Shopify charge extra for non-Shopify payments?

Yes. Using a third-party gateway adds a surcharge on top of the gateway's own fees: 2% (Basic), 1% (Shopify) or 0.5% (Advanced).
