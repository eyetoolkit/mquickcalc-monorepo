How Revolut pricing works

Conversions use the interbank rate plus an FX markup when you're past your monthly free allowance. Revolut additionally charges fixed fees on some transfer types and currencies. The defaults above match a common Standard-plan setup.

How Revolut fees work

Revolut charges a markup fee on international transfers and currency conversions, typically 0.4-3% depending on your plan (Standard, Plus, Premium, Metal). Some routes also have a fixed fee per transaction.

The hidden cost is the exchange rate spread. Revolut uses the interbank rate as its base but then applies the markup. For large transfers, even a 1% markup can add up significantly — compare rates with Wise (TransferWise) or OFX for bigger amounts.

Revolut also offers fee-free transfers at certain times of day for Plus and Premium customers. The weekday mid-market rate window (typically 9am-5pm local time) has the lowest markup.

Frequently asked questions

Does Revolut charge a fee for currency conversion?

Within the monthly free allowance, conversions use the interbank rate with no markup. Above it, Revolut applies a percentage markup on top of the live rate.

How much can I convert free on Revolut?

Standard plans typically include around €1,000 a month of no-markup FX; amounts beyond that incur the standard markup. Check your plan's current allowance.
