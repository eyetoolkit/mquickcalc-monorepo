The US international sale fee

For US sellers, eBay adds an international fee (commonly 1.65%) when the buyer is outside the US, on top of the standard final value fee. Other markets have their own structure — UK and Germany charge a percentage plus a fixed amount per sale. Category rates can differ, so check the fee table for your category.

How eBay fees work

eBay charges an Insertion fee for listing (free for most categories within monthly free listing allowances) and a Final Value Fee (a percentage of the sale price including shipping). Managed Payments adds payment processing fees.

Frequently asked questions

What is eBay's international fee for US sellers?

An extra percentage (commonly 1.65%) charged on top of the final value fee when a US seller's sale is to an international buyer.

Do eBay UK and Germany fees differ from the US?

Yes. UK typically charges a percentage plus a fixed amount per sale, and Germany a similar structure — rates also vary by category. Always confirm the current fee table for your market.
