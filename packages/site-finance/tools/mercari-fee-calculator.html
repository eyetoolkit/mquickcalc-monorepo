Mercari fee basics

Mercari charges a selling fee per sale plus any applicable payment processing fees. Because buyers usually pay shipping through the platform, the amount you enter below is the item price you set.

How Mercari fees work

10% of sale price. No listing fees. Payment processing is included in the 10% fee. Mercari is mobile-first with both shipping and local pickup options. Fast-growing in apparel and home goods.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Mercari charge sellers?

The model has changed across markets and years; a common setup is a percentage selling fee plus payment processing. The defaults here are a reasonable starting point, not a guarantee.

Is listing on Mercari free?

Yes, listing is free; fees are charged when an item sells.
