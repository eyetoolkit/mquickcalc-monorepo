How Kickstarter fees work

Kickstarter charges a platform fee (typically 5%) plus payment processing — a percentage of each pledge plus a small per-pledge amount. If a campaign isn't funded, no fees are charged.

How Kickstarter fees work

Kickstarter charges a flat 5% platform fee on all funds raised, plus payment processing fees (typically 3-5%) depending on whether you use Stripe or PayPal. The processing fee structure varies by country and currency.

Backer-pledge refunds also incur fees. If you refund a pledge, the original payment processing fee is not returned to you — only Kickstarter's 5% is refunded. This is why accurate net-profit estimation matters before launching your campaign.

Successful campaigns also have disbursement timing to consider: funds are typically held for 14 days after the campaign ends before being sent to your bank account. Factor this into your cash flow planning.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Kickstarter take?

Typically 5% platform fee plus payment processing of roughly 3% + a small per-pledge fee, charged only on funded campaigns.

Are Kickstarter fees refunded if a campaign fails?

No fees are charged on a failed campaign — backers are refunded and the creator owes nothing.
