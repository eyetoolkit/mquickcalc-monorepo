Fee model note

Patreon earns a platform fee on membership income and separate payment processing fees may apply per charge. Whether processing fees are passed to members depends on the creator's settings and plan. Defaults above (10% + 2.9% + $0.30, the rate for creators who joined from August 2025) are common approximations — edit them to match your situation.

How Patreon fees work

A creator sees the membership money before fees; Patreon deducts its platform percentage and, where applicable, payment processing per transaction before the payout lands. Actual take-home therefore varies with your plan, the members' card networks, currency conversion and your country.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does Patreon take?

Patreon charges new creators a 10% platform fee, plus payment processing of about 2.9% + $0.30 per pledge. Creators who joined before August 2025 keep legacy rates of roughly 5%, 8% or 12% depending on plan, so check your own dashboard before relying on a single number.

Do creators pay for payment processing?

It depends on plan and settings — some creators pass processing to members, some absorb it. Adjust the editable fields to reflect your setup.
