Fee model note

Patreon earns a platform fee on membership income and separate payment processing fees may apply per charge. Whether processing fees are passed to members depends on the creator's settings and plan. Defaults above (5% + 2.9% + $0.30) are common approximations — edit them to match your situation.

How Patreon fees work

A creator sees the membership money before fees; Patreon deducts its platform percentage and, where applicable, payment processing per transaction before the payout lands. Actual take-home therefore varies with your plan, the members' card networks, currency conversion and your country.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does Patreon take?

Patreon currently charges a platform fee in the range of roughly 5%, plus potential payment processing fees. Because the exact fees changed across Patreon's pricing history, confirm the current rates on Patreon's site.

Do creators pay for payment processing?

It depends on plan and settings — some creators pass processing to members, some absorb it. Adjust the editable fields to reflect your setup.
