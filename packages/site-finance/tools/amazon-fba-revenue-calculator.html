Where an FBA sale's money goes

Amazon takes a referral fee (a % of the sale, ~15% across many categories) plus an FBA fulfillment fee for picking, packing and shipping. Your own costs are the product cost and sending inventory into the warehouse. What's left before Amazon's payment settlements is your profit.

How Amazon FBA fees work

FBA fees include: Referral fee (typically 15%), Fulfillment fee (per unit based on size and weight), Monthly storage fee, and Inbound shipping fee. This calculator accounts for all major FBA costs.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Amazon FBA charge to sell?

Typically a referral fee near 15% plus an FBA fulfillment fee based on size and weight. Costs vary by category and change annually — always check the current fee table.

What's a good FBA profit margin?

After all fees sellers often target 20–30% net. Use the ROI result to sanity-check a product before sourcing stock.
