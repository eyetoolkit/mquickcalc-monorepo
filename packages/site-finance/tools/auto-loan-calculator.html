How car loan amortization works

Each monthly payment covers both interest (calculated on the remaining balance) and principal (paying down the loan). Early payments are mostly interest; later payments are mostly principal. Our formula uses the standard amortization equation: M = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)
