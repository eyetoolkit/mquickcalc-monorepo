The formula

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit, where contribution = price − variable cost. Add a target to cover it on top: (fixed + target) ÷ contribution. Fixed costs typically include rent, software and salaries; variable costs scale with each unit sold.

How break-even is calculated

Break-even point = Fixed Costs divided by (Price minus Variable Cost per Unit). The contribution margin (how much each sale contributes to covering fixed costs) is the key number. At break-even, profit equals zero.

Frequently asked questions

How many units must I sell to break even?

Divide total fixed costs by the profit left on each unit after variable cost. With $1,000 fixed cost and $15 contribution, you need 67 units (~67).

What's contribution margin?

Price minus variable cost. It's the dollars each sale contributes toward covering fixed costs and, beyond break-even, profit.
