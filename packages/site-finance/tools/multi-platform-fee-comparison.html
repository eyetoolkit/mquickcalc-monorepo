Multi-Platform Fee Comparison

ONE input, SIX results. Enter your sale amount once and instantly compare what you'd net on every major platform. The only calculator you need to decide where to sell.

How the comparison works

Each platform uses a different fee structure: percentage of sale, fixed per-item, tiered by category, or combinations thereof. This tool applies each platform's standard rate to your input sale amount. Some platforms have category-specific exceptions (e.g., eBay Books, Amazon Jewelry) — check individual calculators for fine-grained numbers.
