Gumroad fee model

Gumroad charges a service fee per sale (commonly 10%) and payment processing (roughly 2.9% + $0.30) applies. Depending on your settings the processing may land on the creator or be added to the buyer's price. The defaults above are the common "creator absorbs processing" view.

How Gumroad fees work

Gumroad takes a flat 10% fee (includes payment processing on most plans). No listing fees — you pay only when you sell. Gumroad handles checkout, delivery, tax collection, and customer support for digital products.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Gumroad take per sale?

Usually a flat service fee near 10% plus payment processing. So a $15 sale might net around $12.5 after about $2.5 in combined fees.

Can I make buyers pay the processing fee?

Gumroad offers settings affecting whether processing is covered by you or passed to the buyer — which is exactly why you can dial the fee fields above.
