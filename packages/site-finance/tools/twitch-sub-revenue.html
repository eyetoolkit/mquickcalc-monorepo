How Twitch subscription revenue is split

A streamer and Twitch share the subscription fee. The classic split is 50/50 (streamer keeps $2.50 of a $4.99 Tier 1). Larger or qualifying channels can move to a 70/30 split, which changes every payout up to a per-sub revenue cap.

Use the editable share to model either split, then combine with Bits income for a fuller picture of channel earnings.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Twitch streamer make per sub?

At a 50/50 split, roughly $2.50 per Tier 1, $5.00 per Tier 2 and $12.50 per Tier 3. At 70/30 it's higher for qualifying channels.

Do Prime subs make the streamer money?

Yes — a Prime sub is effectively a Tier 1 sub included with Amazon Prime, and the streamer is paid the same split as a paid Tier 1 sub would produce.
