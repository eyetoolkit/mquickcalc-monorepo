How this profit calculator works

Profit is price − cost. Profit margin is profit ÷ price — the share of revenue that is profit. Markup is profit ÷ cost — how much you added on top of cost. The two percentages answer different questions, which is why buyers and sellers often disagree: the buyer thinks in margin, the seller in markup.

What to use it for

Check whether a product clears your desired margin before you list it, compare two price points, or price a service against your cost basis. The target-margin box tells you the exact price that hits a margin you have in mind.
