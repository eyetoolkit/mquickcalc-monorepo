Why freelancers charge more

You're paying for taxes, healthcare, equipment, downtime and non-billable work. Grossing up your target income by the tax rate and adding overhead ensures the hours you bill actually deliver the take-home you planned for.

How to set freelance rates

Hourly rate calculation: (desired annual salary plus overhead plus business costs) divided by billable hours per year. Full-time has ~2080 hours but freelancers average only 1200-1600 billable hours due to admin, marketing, and downtime.

Frequently asked questions

How many billable hours does a freelancer work?

Realistically 1,000–1,500 a year — the rest goes to admin, marketing and unpaid work. A 40-hour salaried equivalent is ~2,000 hours.

Should my freelance rate include taxes?

Yes — divide your required net by (1 − tax rate) so the gross rate covers what you'll owe. Many freelancers budget 20–35% for taxes.
