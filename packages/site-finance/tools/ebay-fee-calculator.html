Fee basics

Most eBay categories charge a final value fee on the total amount (item + shipping + applicable taxes) plus a flat per-order fee. Above a certain amount per order, the excess is taxed at a lower rate in many setups. In seller-managed payments the payment processing is already folded into these numbers.

How eBay seller fees work

When an item sells, eBay deducts a final value fee as a percentage of the sale total plus a small fixed per-order fee. Add-ons like additional listing fees, promoted listings or international transaction fees can apply on top. Reserve the technical default threshold tier for high-ticket sales.

Frequently asked questions

How much does eBay take on a $100 sale?

With a 13.6% rate on a $100 sale (13.6% + $0.40 per-order fee), roughly $14.00 in fees, leaving about $86.00 before shipping costs and any extra fees.

Does eBay charge a listing fee?

A limited number of free listings are included each month, after which insertion fees apply. Most advertised margins are driven by the final value fee instead.
