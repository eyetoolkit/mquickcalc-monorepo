Net vs. gross

Net is the price before VAT; gross is the price with VAT included. Adding VAT multiplies net by (1 + rate); removing it divides gross by (1 + rate). Small-rate differences compound on large invoices, so it pays to get the direction right.

How EU VAT works

VAT = Net price times tax rate (varies 17-27% by country). One Stop Shop (OSS) simplifies cross-border filing. Low-value goods (under 150 euro) may use IOSS for quick EU imports. Reverse charge applies to B2B intra-EU sales.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add VAT to a price?

Multiply the net price by 1 + the rate. At 20%, a £100 net price becomes £120 gross.

How do I remove VAT from a total?

Divide the gross price by 1 + the rate. A £120 gross total at 20% is £100 net, with £20 VAT.
