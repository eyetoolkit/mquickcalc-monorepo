A note on accuracy

These defaults reflect commonly cited Etsy fees, but totals are approximate. Regulatory operating fees differ by country and can be 0% in some locations, and off-site ads only apply to orders attributed to Etsy's off-site ads program.

How Etsy seller fees work

Etsy deducts fees from each sale before you receive your payout. The main ones are a transaction fee on the item price, a payment processing fee, a listing fee per active listing, and in some countries a regulatory operating fee. Orders sent through off-site ads also incur an ad fee.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy take per sale?

It varies. With a $25 item and defaults above, roughly $2.93–$3.00 is deducted in fees, leaving about $22.07–$22.08 before off-site ads. Adjust the editable fields to match your exact situation.

Is the off-site ads fee always charged?

No. It only applies when the sale is attributed to Etsy's off-site ads program. If you sell all your items through your own traffic, keep it at 0%.

Why is my net amount different from this?

Shipping fees, discounts, refunds, currency conversion and regional fees can all change the outcome. Treat this as a planning estimate.
