What the comparison shows

Annual billing improves cash flow and retention but the 2-months-free discount usually lowers total net unless you raise prices. Selling direct with Stripe removes the platform fee entirely — worth it once your audience is big enough that you can manage memberships yourself.

How Patreon fees work

Patreon takes a platform fee (5-12% depending on your plan) plus payment processing fees (typically 2.9% plus 0.30 per transaction). Fees are charged on each pledge, not just what Patreon keeps. You receive the pledge minus all fees.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does Patreon take?

Patreon charges a platform fee (commonly 5%) plus payment processing on top of each transaction. The exact processing rate depends on the member's card and country.

Is Patreon annual billing better for creators?

It helps cash flow and retention, but the 2-months-free offer lowers annual gross — the net effect depends on your member count and average pledge. Compare both columns above.
