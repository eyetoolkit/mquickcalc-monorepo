Home Insurance

4 free calculators & converters. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded.

Home Insurance Cost Estimator

Estimate annual homeowners insurance premiums based on your state, home size, age, and deductible choice. Includes common discounts.

Renters Insurance Coverage Calculator

Estimate how much renters insurance coverage you need and what it might cost per year. Covers personal property, liability, and additional living expenses.

Flood Insurance Cost Estimator

Estimate annual flood insurance premiums based on your FEMA NFIP zone, building height, and replacement cost. Flood insurance is separate from home insurance.

Umbrella Liability Calculator

How much umbrella insurance do you need? Calculate based on your total assets and annual income. Umbrella coverage kicks in when your auto/home liability limits max out.
