mQuickCalc

Health & Pet Insurance

4 free calculators & converters. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded.

Health Insurance Premium Estimator

Ballpark monthly health insurance premiums by metal tier (Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum), age, state, and family size. Includes government subsidy estimate.

Medigap (Medicare Supplement) Cost Estimator

Estimate monthly premiums for Medicare Supplement plans A through N. Medigap covers what Medicare Parts A and B don't pay.

Long-Term Care Insurance Cost Calculator

Estimate annual premiums for long-term care insurance (nursing home, assisted living, home health care). Compare buying at 55 vs 65 to see the early-bird savings.

Pet Insurance Value Calculator

Estimate monthly pet insurance costs for dogs and cats by breed, age, and coverage level. Also calculates whether pet insurance is actually worth it vs self-insuring.
