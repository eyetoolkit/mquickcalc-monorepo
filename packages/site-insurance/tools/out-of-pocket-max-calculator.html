Frequently asked questions

What counts toward the out-of-pocket maximum?

For most plans, your deductible, copays and coinsurance on covered in-network care count toward the out-of-pocket maximum. Your monthly premium never counts. Out-of-network spending may be excluded or tracked against a separate maximum.

Does the plan pay 100% after I hit the out-of-pocket max?

For covered in-network services, yes: once you reach the out-of-pocket maximum, the plan pays 100% of allowed amounts for the rest of the plan year. Services that are not covered, or penalties for using out-of-network providers, can still cost you money.

How is this different from a deductible calculator?

A deductible only tracks the amount you pay before the plan starts sharing costs. The out-of-pocket maximum is the yearly ceiling on your total cost sharing, including deductible, copays and coinsurance. This tool follows both, so you can see when the cap kicks in.
