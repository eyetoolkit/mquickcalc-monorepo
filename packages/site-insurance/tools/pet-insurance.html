Frequently asked questions

Is pet insurance a waste of money?

It depends on your situation. Pet insurance is most valuable for dogs (higher vet costs), purebreds (genetic conditions), and emergency coverage. If you'd struggle to pay a $2,000 vet bill, insurance is worth it. If you'd comfortably cover emergency costs, you might be better off self-insuring by saving the monthly premium.

What does pet insurance actually cover?

Most plans cover accidents (broken bones, poisoning) and illnesses (cancer, infections). Wellness (annual checkups, vaccines, teeth cleaning) is usually a rider. IMPORTANT: Almost all policies have a 14-day waiting period, and pre-existing conditions are excluded forever — so insure your pet while they're young and healthy.

Are there exclusions I should watch for?

Yes, read the fine print carefully: pre-existing conditions (any condition your pet had before the waiting period ends) are almost always excluded; many hereditary conditions (hip dysplasia, heart disease) in certain breeds may be excluded or have longer waiting periods; and routine preventative care is typically a separate add-on.
