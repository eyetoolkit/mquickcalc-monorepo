Frequently asked questions

What is the DIME method?

DIME stands for Debts, Income (×10), Mortgage, and Education. It's a simple formula popularized by life insurance experts: add up all debts, multiply annual income by 10, add remaining mortgage balance, and add projected college costs for kids. The result is a practical starting point.

Term vs whole life — which should I choose?

Term life is dramatically cheaper because it only pays out if you die during the term (10, 20, or 30 years). Whole life is permanent and has a cash-value component, but costs 3-5× more. For most young families, a large 20-30 year term policy is the better value.

Is this estimate accurate?

Our rate tables are based on industry averages for healthy non-smokers. Your actual rate depends on underwriting factors we can't know: medical history, family health, occupation risk, hobbies (skydiving, etc.), and the specific carrier's pricing. Always get multiple actual quotes.
