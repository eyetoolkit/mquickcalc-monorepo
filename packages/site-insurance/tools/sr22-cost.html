Frequently asked questions

What is an SR-22?

An SR-22 is a certificate from your insurance company proving you meet the state's minimum liability requirements. It's required after serious violations (DUI, reckless driving, driving without insurance). Your insurance company files it with the DMV, and you pay a filing fee ($25-$75/year depending on state).

Does an SR-22 make my insurance more expensive?

Yes — significantly. A DUI typically doubles or triples your premium. The SR-22 form itself costs very little ($25-75/year), but the insurance surcharge is the real cost. You'll carry this penalty for 3-10 years depending on your state and violation.

What's non-owner SR-22 insurance?

If you don't own a car but need an SR-22 (common after a DUI where you drove someone else's car), you can buy a non-owner policy. It only covers liability when you're driving someone else's car. It's cheaper than a regular policy but still has the high-risk surcharge.
