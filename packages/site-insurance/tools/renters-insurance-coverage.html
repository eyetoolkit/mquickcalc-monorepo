How this coverage estimator works

It reads the dollar values from your item list (the number before the "$" on each line) and sums them into a property total. It then compares that total to your personal property limit. If your belongings are worth more than the limit, the difference is your gap — the amount you would absorb out of pocket after a total loss. Liability is reported separately because it protects you from claims others make against you, not from loss of your own stuff.

What to use it for

Check you are not underinsured after a move or when you buy expensive electronics, decide whether to raise your property limit or add a scheduled-personal-property endorsement for high-value items, and compare a couple of liability tiers before you sign.
