How this calculator works

It turns your annual income into a target monthly benefit using the replacement rate you choose, then subtracts any disability benefit you already receive. The remainder is the extra monthly cover to buy; multiplied by twelve it is your annual coverage gap. The waiting-period buffer is simply your essential monthly expenses times the number of months before benefits begin, and the premium range is a rough 1–3% of income planning figure.

What to use it for

Use it before a job change, when comparing an employer group plan with an individual policy, or when deciding whether to top up an existing benefit. Remember that group plans may cap monthly payments and exclude bonus income, so check your plan documents and get a real quote — this is a planning estimate, not insurance advice.
