Frequently asked questions

What does a travel insurance cost estimator show?

It estimates a single-trip policy premium from trip cost, traveler age, destination and coverage level. The number is a ballpark to help you weigh cost against trip price, not a bound quote.

When should I buy travel insurance?

Ideally within a few days of booking, so cancellation coverage applies from the start. The estimator helps you judge whether the cost is worth it for your trip.

Does travel insurance cover trip cancellation?

Most comprehensive plans do, for covered reasons such as illness or carrier disruption. The calculator estimates cost; the policy wording defines what is actually covered.
