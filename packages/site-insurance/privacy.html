Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-14

No personal data

Cover does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information you enter into our calculators. All calculations happen in your browser. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, address, or insurance policy details.

What we log

Like most websites, our servers automatically log basic technical information for security and performance: IP address, browser type, device, time of request, and the page you visited. These logs are deleted after 30 days and are never sold or shared with third parties.

Cookies

We use essential cookies only — session tokens, accessibility preferences. We do not use advertising cookies. Our third-party ad provider (Google AdSense) may place their own cookies; see Google's privacy policy.

Third-party services

Cover uses: Google Analytics (anonymous site usage), Google AdSense (advertising), Cloudflare (CDN and security). Each has their own privacy policies linked above.

We don't do lead generation

Unlike many insurance comparison sites, Cover will never ask for your contact information and will never sell your data to brokers or carriers. If you see a "get a quote" button anywhere on our site, that's an ad — the advertiser collects your information, not us.
