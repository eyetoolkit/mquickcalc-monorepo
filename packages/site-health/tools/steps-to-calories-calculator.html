How steps convert to calories

The conversion depends on your weight and how vigorously you move. Heavier people burn more calories per step (they're moving more mass). Pace affects it too — brisk walking and running both use more energy per step than strolling. Your smartphone's activity tracker likely uses a similar formula.

Frequently asked questions

How many calories does 10,000 steps burn?

Roughly 300-500 kcal for an average adult, but it depends on your weight, pace, and terrain. Heavier people and faster walkers burn more per step.

Is walking or running better per step?

Running burns more per step because it moves more mass with greater force, but walking more steps in the same time can match the total. Intensity and duration both matter.

Why does my weight change the result so much?

Calories burned scale with the mass you move. A heavier body expends more energy for the same distance, which is why the calculator asks for your weight.
