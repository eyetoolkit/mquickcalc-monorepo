How this heart-rate calculator works

It estimates your maximum with 207 − 0.7 × age, then applies the Karvonen method: each zone is a percentage of your heart-rate reserve (max minus resting) added back to your resting rate. Without a resting number it falls back to a percentage of max, which is the simpler and less individualized version.

What to use it for

Set a target heart-rate band for a run, cycle, or cardio class so you know whether you are in fat-burning, endurance, or intensity territory. It is a planning tool, not a diagnosis.
