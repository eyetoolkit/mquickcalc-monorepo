Sleep Cycle Calculator

Plan your sleep around 90-minute cycles so you wake at the end of a light-sleep phase — refreshed instead of groggy. Enter your target wake time or bed time below.

—
—
About sleep cycles: A full sleep cycle lasts ~90 minutes and alternates between light sleep, deep sleep, and REM. Waking at the end of a cycle (during light sleep) leaves you feeling more refreshed than waking during deep sleep. Aim for 4–6 cycles (6–9 hours) per night. Adults typically need 7–9 hours.

How it works

  • Each sleep cycle lasts 90 minutes. Count backward from your target time in 90-minute increments.
  • Add a fall-asleep buffer (default 15 min) to account for the time it takes to actually fall asleep after going to bed.
  • The schedule shows options from 3 cycles (4.5 h) to 8 cycles (12 h). Fewer than 4 cycles is not recommended for most adults.
  • The highlighted rows (4–6 cycles) represent the recommended range for most adults.

Estimates are for orientation only. Sleep needs vary by age, health, and lifestyle. This tool does not provide medical advice.
