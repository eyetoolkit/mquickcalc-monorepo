How the Widmark formula works

BAC = grams of alcohol ÷ (body water fraction × body weight in grams) − 0.015 × hours elapsed. The body metabolizes roughly 15mg% of alcohol per hour — about one standard drink per hour.

About the BAC (Blood Alcohol)

Estimate your blood alcohol content (BAC) from drinks consumed, body weight, and time elapsed. Uses the Widmark formula. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so nothing you enter is uploaded or stored.
