Pace Calculator

Convert running or cycling pace between minutes per mile, minutes per kilometer, miles per hour, and kilometers per hour — all four units update instantly as you type.

How pace conversion works

All pace units interconvert via simple math: pace-min/unit = 60 / speed-unit/hour. Handles min/mile, min/km, mph, km/h. Pace = time per distance; speed = distance per time. Runners think in pace, non-runners in speed.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the results?

Formulas used here are validated in population studies but cannot account for individual genetics, body composition, or medical conditions. Treat results as a starting point, not a medical diagnosis.

Should I consult a professional?

If you have specific health concerns, existing conditions, or are planning significant lifestyle changes, consult a qualified healthcare provider (doctor, dietitian, physiologist).

How often should I recalculate?

Recalculate when your weight changes more than 5%, activity level shifts significantly, or every 3-6 months for routine monitoring. Results are snapshots — your body changes daily.

About the Pace

Instantly convert running pace between minutes per mile, minutes per kilometer, miles per hour, and kilometers per hour. Perfect for runners and cyclists. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so nothing you enter is uploaded or stored.

How to use the Pace

  1. Minutes per mile (min/mile) — your minutes per mile in min/mile.
  2. Minutes per kilometer (min/km) — your minutes per kilometer in min/km.
  3. Miles per hour (mph) — enter your miles per hour.
  4. Kilometers per hour (km/h) — your kilometers per hour in km/h.
