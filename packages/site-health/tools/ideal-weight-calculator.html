How these formulas work

Devine (1974), Robinson (1983), and Miller (1990) were all developed from population data and are still used by hospitals for drug-dose calculations. They all assume a medium frame; we apply ±10% for small/large frames. The BMI range is the WHO standard healthy range.

No single formula accounts for muscle mass, ethnicity, or genetics — they are population estimates, not targets.
