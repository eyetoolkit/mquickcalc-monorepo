How body type scoring works

We combine three ratios: wrist-to-height (frame size), waist-to-shoulder (upper body shape), and waist-to-hip (lower body shape). Each ratio contributes weighted points to ectomorph / mesomorph / endomorph categories. The highest-scoring category is your primary type; second-place is your secondary. Most people are a blend.
