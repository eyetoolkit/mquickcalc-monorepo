How body type scoring works

We combine three ratios: wrist-to-height (frame size), waist-to-shoulder (upper body shape), and waist-to-hip (lower body shape). Each ratio contributes weighted points to ectomorph / mesomorph / endomorph categories. The highest-scoring category is your primary type; second-place is your secondary. Most people are a blend.

About the Body Type

Discover your body type (ectomorph, mesomorph, endomorph) based on wrist, waist, hip, shoulder measurements and visual cues. The tool runs entirely in your browser, so nothing you enter is uploaded or stored.

How to use the Body Type

  1. Wrist circumference (cm) — your wrist circumference in cm.
  2. Natural waist (cm) — your natural waist in cm.
  3. Hip circumference (cm) — your hip circumference in cm.
  4. Shoulder width (cm, back level) — your shoulder width in cm, back level.
  5. Height (cm) — your height in cm.
