What sleep debt means

Sleep debt accumulates when you sleep less than your body needs. Research shows that while you can partially catch up by sleeping longer on weekends, chronic debt causes cognitive decline, metabolic disruption, and weakened immunity. Recovery is linear: about 1 hour of extra sleep per 1 hour of deficit, spread across several days.
