Calories Burned by Distance

Estimate calories burned walking, running, or cycling a given distance. Based on MET values and body weight — no pace or time needed.

Note: These are estimates based on average MET values. Actual calories depend on pace, terrain, and individual metabolism.

How distance-based calories work

Heavier bodies burn more calories at same distance. Walking ~0.6 kcal/kg/km. Running ~1.0 kcal/kg/km. Running burns ~2× walking calories per km due to impact and greater muscle engagement. Population averages only.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the results?

Formulas used here are validated in population studies but cannot account for individual genetics, body composition, or medical conditions. Treat results as a starting point, not a medical diagnosis.

Should I consult a professional?

If you have specific health concerns, existing conditions, or are planning significant lifestyle changes, consult a qualified healthcare provider (doctor, dietitian, physiologist).

How often should I recalculate?

Recalculate when your weight changes more than 5%, activity level shifts significantly, or every 3-6 months for routine monitoring. Results are snapshots — your body changes daily.
