About mQuickCalc Health — Wellness Calculators

mQuickCalc Health provides free wellness calculators for body metrics, nutrition and fitness. Our approach to formulas, privacy and health information.

What this site is

mQuickCalc Health (health.mquickcalc.com) is a free suite of wellness, fitness and nutrition calculators — BMI, BMR, TDEE, calorie targets, hydration, VO₂ max, pace and more. Every calculation runs locally in your browser, so your numbers stay yours.

Why you can trust the numbers

  • Formulas reference established published methods (e.g. Mifflin-St Jeor, US Navy method, Karvonen).
  • Each tool explains what it estimates and its limitations.
  • Results are estimates for general wellness, not medical diagnoses.
  • Private by design — no account, no paywall — calculator inputs stay on your device.

Privacy by design

Every calculator runs in your browser. We do not store the numbers you enter on our servers, and as of this writing we run no third-party advertising. See our Privacy Policy.

Who runs this?
