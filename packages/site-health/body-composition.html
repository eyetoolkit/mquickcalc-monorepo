Body Composition

9 free calculators & converters. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded.

Available calculators

BMI Calculator

Free BMI calculator: estimate your Body Mass Index and category instantly. Uses the standard WHO BMI formula. No sign-up required.

BMR Calculator (Mifflin-St Jeor)

Free BMR calculator using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Calculate how many calories your body burns at complete rest. Accurate for most adults.

Body Fat Percentage Calculator

Free body fat calculator using the US Navy method. Enter height and body circumference measurements to estimate body fat percentage — no calipers needed.

Body Surface Area (BSA) Calculator

Calculate Body Surface Area (BSA) using 5 formulas: Du Bois, Mosteller, Haycock, Gehan-George, Boyd. Used in medicine, nutrition, and fitness.

Body Type Calculator

Discover your body type (ectomorph, mesomorph, endomorph) based on wrist, waist, hip, shoulder measurements and visual cues.

Reverse BMI Calculator

Enter your height and target BMI to find your ideal goal weight. Works for both metric (cm/kg) and imperial (ft/lbs) units.

Lean Body Mass Calculator

Free lean body mass calculator. Enter your weight and body fat percentage to estimate how much of your weight is lean tissue vs fat mass.

Ideal Weight Calculator

Estimate your ideal healthy weight range based on height, gender, and body frame. Uses three evidence-based formulas (Devine, Robinson, Miller).

Waist-to-Height Ratio Calculator

Free waist-to-height ratio calculator. More accurate than BMI for health risk assessment in many people — measure waist and height only.
