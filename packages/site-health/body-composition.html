Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my BMI?

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)^2. A BMI of 18.5-24.9 is healthy, 25-29.9 is overweight, and 30+ is obese. The BMI calculator handles kg/lbs and metric/imperial.

How do I measure my body fat percentage?

The US Navy method uses neck, waist and hip circumference. The body fat calculator estimates it in seconds. Healthy ranges are 14-20% for men and 21-33% for women.

What is a healthy waist-to-height ratio?

Aim for under 0.5, your waist should measure less than half your height. The waist-to-height calculator checks your risk level and is considered more accurate than BMI alone.
