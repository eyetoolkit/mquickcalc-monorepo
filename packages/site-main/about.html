About mQuickCalc

Fast, free everyday calculators and unit converters — built to be simple and private.

What this is

mQuickCalc is a personal, independent project providing everyday calculators and unit converters — things like date and time math, temperature, distance and unit conversions. It's built for speed, clarity and simplicity, with no sign-up and no paywall.

How it works

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are processed locally by JavaScript on your device and are never sent to or stored on our servers.

A family of sites

mQuickCalc is organized into focused sub-sites so you can find the right tool fast:

  • finance.mquickcalc.com — marketplace fees, creator income, business & pricing calculators.
  • health.mquickcalc.com — fitness, nutrition and everyday wellness calculators.

The main domain here at mquickcalc.com will be home to general everyday calculators and converters.
