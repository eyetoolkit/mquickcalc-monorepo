How it works

Area units are the square of length units, which is why the conversion factors look large: a square foot is 0.3048 m squared, or 0.09290304 m². Converting area means multiplying by the square of the length factor, not the length factor itself — a mistake that produces answers off by an order of magnitude.

Square feet to square meters is the most common conversion in property: multiply square feet by 0.0929. A 1,000 ft² apartment is about 93 m². Going the other way, multiply square meters by 10.764.

The acre and the hectare are both land units from different systems. A hectare is 10,000 m² — a square 100 m on each side — while an acre is 4,046.86 m². One hectare is about 2.47 acres, so a hectare is roughly two and a half times an acre.

When measuring a room for flooring, remember that area scales with the square of linear error. If you measure a wall as 5.1 m when it is actually 5.0 m, the area is overstated by about 4%, not 2%. Always order a little extra material for cutting waste regardless.

Real-world applications

Real-world: US real estate uses sq ft; metric uses sq m (2,000 ft² = 185.8 m²). Land: 1 hectare = 2.47 acres. Roofing contractors use squares (100 ft² = 9.29 m²).

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet are in a square meter?

One square meter is 10.7639 square feet. To convert, multiply square meters by 10.7639 — a 90 m² apartment is about 969 ft². To go the other way, multiply square feet by 0.0929.

What is the difference between an acre and a hectare?

A hectare is 10,000 m² and an acre is 4,046.86 m², so one hectare equals about 2.47 acres. Hectares are used in metric countries and in most scientific contexts; acres remain standard in US and UK property and land listings.

Why is the conversion factor not just the length factor?

Because area is two-dimensional. A foot is 0.3048 m, but a square foot is 0.3048 × 0.3048 = 0.0929 m². Using the length factor for an area conversion is the single most common error and throws the result off by more than 3×.

How do I convert acres to square feet?

One acre is exactly 43,560 square feet. Multiply acres by 43,560: a 2.5-acre plot is 108,900 ft², or about 1.01 hectares.

Should I add waste when ordering flooring?

Yes — typically 5–10% for straight layouts and up to 15% for diagonal or patterned installs. The converter gives you the exact area; it cannot account for cutting waste, board direction or room irregularities. For a 50 m² room with 10% waste, order 55 m² of flooring. When in doubt, round up — leftover material can be stored for future repairs.

What is a "square" in roofing?

In US construction, a "square" is 100 square feet (9.29 m²) and is the standard unit for quoting roofing materials. A 2,000 ft² roof requires 20 squares of shingles. Roofing contractors typically add 10–15% waste for cut edges and starter strips, so a 20-square roof might require 22–23 squares of material. This converter can help you translate between the roofing square system and metric area measurements when working with international suppliers.

How do I convert square meters to acres for farmland?

Divide square meters by 4,046.86 to get acres, or multiply acres by 4,046.86 to get square meters. For quick mental math, 1 hectare (10,000 m²) is approximately 2.47 acres — so 5 hectares is roughly 12.35 acres. Agricultural land in the US, UK, and Canada is typically listed in acres, while most other countries use hectares. If you are comparing land prices per unit area internationally, convert everything to acres or hectares first before comparing dollar-per-acre figures.
