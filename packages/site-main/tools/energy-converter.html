How it works

All energy conversions route through the joule (J), the SI unit. One calorie — the small gram-calorie — equals 4.184 J. But food "calories" are actually kilocalories (kcal), or 1,000 small calories, so 1 food Calorie ≈ 4,184 J = 4.184 kJ.

Kilowatt-hours (kWh) are energy units too, even though the name looks like power. 1 kWh = 3,600,000 J = 3.6 MJ. A 100 W light bulb left on for 10 hours uses 1 kWh. Your electricity bill charges you per kWh.

The BTU (British Thermal Unit) is the amount of energy needed to heat one pound of water by one degree Fahrenheit — about 1,055 J. It's still used for natural gas pricing and furnace ratings in the US. 1 kWh ≈ 3,412 BTU, 1 kcal ≈ 3.968 BTU.

Real-world applications

Exercise tracking: Running 5 km burns about 300-400 kcal (food calories = kilocalories). 350 kcal = 1,465 kJ = 1,384 BTU. That is about a 40-watt bulb running for 10 hours.

HVAC sizing: Furnaces are rated in BTU/h. A 2,000 sq ft home needs 60,000-100,000 BTU/h. 1 kW = 3,412 BTU/h. Central air conditioners use tons - 1 ton = 12,000 BTU/h = 3.517 kW.

The calorie confusion

A food Calorie (capital C) is a kilocalorie = 1,000 small calories = 4,184 joules. The physics calorie heats 1 gram of water by 1°C. This is why fitness apps use kJ and food labels use Calories.

Frequently asked questions

How many kcal should I eat per day?

Average is 2,000 kcal for women, 2,500 kcal for men. These are food Calories (kilocalories), not the small gram-calories used in chemistry. 1 food Cal = 1 kcal = 4.184 kJ.

What is a kWh?

Kilowatt-hour is energy, not power. 1 kWh = 3.6 million joules. A 100 W bulb left on for 10 hours uses 1 kWh. Your electricity bill charges per kWh.

BTU vs calorie - which is bigger?

1 BTU is about 252 cal or 0.252 kcal or 1,055 J. BTU is the amount of heat to warm 1 lb water by 1 degree F. It is still used for gas and furnaces in the US.
