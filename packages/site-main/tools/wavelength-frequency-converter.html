How it works

Wavelength and frequency are inversely proportional, related by the wave speed formula: c = λf, where c is the speed of light (299,792,458 m/s in vacuum). Rearranged: λ = c/f and f = c/λ. Higher frequency = shorter wavelength, lower frequency = longer wavelength.

Units: wavelength in meters (m), centimeters (cm), millimeters (mm), or micrometers (µm). Frequency in hertz (Hz), gigahertz (GHz), terahertz (THz). Radio waves (100 MHz = 3 m) → microwaves (10 GHz = 3 cm) → infrared (10 THz = 30 µm) → visible light (400–700 THz) → UV → X-rays → gamma rays.

For sound waves, use speed of sound (~343 m/s in air at 20°C) instead of c. This tool uses speed of light (electromagnetic spectrum). Input either wavelength or frequency — both update in real time.

Real-world applications

Radio and WiFi: FM radio 88-108 MHz (wavelength 3-3.4 meters). WiFi 2.4 GHz uses 12.5 cm waves; 5 GHz uses 6 cm waves. Shorter wavelength = more bandwidth but shorter range.

Visible light: Red light = 650 nm = 461 THz. Blue light = 450 nm = 666 THz. Higher frequency (shorter wavelength) = higher energy per photon.

Frequently asked questions

How are wavelength and frequency related?

Inversely proportional: c = lambda times f, where c = speed of light (299,792,458 m/s). Higher frequency equals shorter wavelength. A 100 MHz radio wave has lambda = 3 m; a 10 GHz microwave has lambda = 3 cm.

Is 5G radio or microwave?

5G uses both sub-6 GHz (lambda about 10-5 cm, microwave band) and mmWave (24-40 GHz, lambda about 12.5-7.5 mm). Higher frequency means more bandwidth but shorter range.

Why is the sky blue?

Sunlight is white (mixed wavelengths). Blue light (lambda about 450 nm) scatters more in atmosphere than red (lambda about 650 nm) - called Rayleigh scattering. At sunset, light travels through more air and blue scatters away, leaving red.
