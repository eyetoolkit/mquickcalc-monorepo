Embed a calculator on your site

Every converter below can be dropped into your own page with one iframe. The tools are free, need no sign-up, and the maths runs in your visitor's browser — no data leaves their device.

How to use: pick a converter, copy the snippet, paste it into your HTML where you want it to appear. The iframe is responsive (100% width) and lazily loaded, so it does not slow your page down. Attribution is appreciated but not required.

Prefer a link?

You can also link straight to any tool page — for example Length Converter, Weight Converter or Temperature Converter.
