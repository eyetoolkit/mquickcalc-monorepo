Why use these converters?

Most conversion tools answer one pair at a time — centimeters to inches, then inches to centimeters as a separate search. Every converter here works the other way: you type one value and the whole table updates at once, so you can read off millimeters and miles without touching another field.

The factors are exact definitions rather than rounded constants. An inch is exactly 2.54 cm, a pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg, and a US gallon is exactly 3.785411784 L. Where two systems disagree — US versus UK gallons, decimal gigabytes versus binary gibibytes — both are listed separately instead of quietly picking one.

That last point matters more than it sounds: a US gallon and an imperial gallon differ by 20%, and a 1 TB drive reports as 931 GB for the same reason. Each converter explains the discrepancy rather than hiding it.
