📏 Length Conversion Guide

Complete guide to length unit conversion — cm to inches, meters to feet, km to miles. Learn exact formulas, real-world use cases, and how the two measurement systems compare.

Length is the most basic of all measurements. Every civilization has needed a way to describe how far apart two things are. Today we live with two competing systems: the metric system (SI, used by 94% of the world) and imperial/customary units (US and a handful of holdouts).

The two systems: imperial vs metric

The metric system uses base-10: 1 km = 1,000 meters = 100,000 cm = 1,000,000 mm. Imperial evolved organically: 12 inches = 1 foot, 3 feet = 1 yard, 1,760 yards = 1 mile. Conversions within imperial are messy, but conversions across systems are exact.

Exact conversion factors

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm — exactly, since 1959
  • 1 foot = 30.48 cm
  • 1 mile = 1.609344 km
  • 1 meter = 3.28084 feet
  • 1 km = 0.621371 miles

Real-world scenarios

At the doctor's: 5'10" = 70 inches x 2.54 = 177.8 cm. Going the other way: 180 cm = 70.87 inches = 5'11".

Driving across borders: US highway 70 mph = 113 km/h. Canada uses 100 km/h ≈ 62 mph.

Real estate: A square foot is (0.3048)^2 = 0.0929 m^2, not 0.3048. This is the most common mistake. A 2,000 ft^2 home is 185.8 m^2, not 609.6 m^2.

How converters handle this

Frequently asked questions

How many cm is an inch?

1 inch = exactly 2.54 cm since 1959. 12 inches = 1 foot = 30.48 cm.

How many miles is a kilometer?

1 km = 0.621371 miles. 1 mile = 1.609344 km.

Is a yard longer than a meter?

No. 1 meter = 1.0936 yards, about 3.37% longer.

Why does the US use inches and feet?

The US kept imperial after Britain switched to metric in 1965. US law allows metric but does not mandate it.
