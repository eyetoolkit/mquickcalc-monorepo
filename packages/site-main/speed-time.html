4 free calculators & converters. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded.

Available calculators

Speed Converter

Convert speed between km/h, mph, knots, m/s, and ft/s. Includes a speed limit reference table for drivers, travelers, and aviation.

Time Converter

Convert time between milliseconds, seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months and years. Handles the awkward month length with the calendar-average year.

Date Difference Calculator

Calculate the exact number of days, weeks, months, and years between two dates. Also find weekdays between dates.

Age Calculator

Calculate your exact age in years, months, and days from your birth date. Also shows your next birthday countdown and life milestone.
